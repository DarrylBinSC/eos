Two Strev AI use cases entered UiPath intake on 2026-09-15. Per v2.5 §5.4 the closest catalog match requires an active AI Feature Pack, which carries a base license of $84,000 per year per environment plus a $10,000 minimum annual credit commitment per Authorized Tenant; the vendor's own illustrative scenarios run $94,000 to $184,000 per year. **Three compounding exposures.** First, v2.5 §6.3 publishes no credit conversion rates and states no standard method exists to translate a credit into token usage, so expected consumption cannot be modeled and only the floor is knowable; overage bills at 1.25x. Second, no Strev line is visible in the FY27 WAM P&L (2026-09-10 Sean deck work) and there is no Strev FY27 AOP change log, so there is no budget home. Third, v2.5 states it creates no contractual obligation and that expansion requires a §14.5 change order; the contract as amended is silent on every AI SKU. **The risk is arrival by accumulation:** individual use case approvals produce a platform commitment nobody decided to make. Mitigation in flight: `05` A49 (written question list to Revnue, merged in 2026-10-05), A44 IDP duplication comparison, C46 SKU confirmation with Amy.
