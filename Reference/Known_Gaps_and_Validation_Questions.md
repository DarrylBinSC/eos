## 7. Validation Questions for Any New Project

1. What is the authoritative current source?
2. What is durable versus time-sensitive?
3. Who owns the outcome?
4. What decision is required?
5. What business benefit is expected?
6. What evidence exists?
7. What is inferred?
8. What has changed since the context file was last validated?
9. Which dependencies are outside Darryl's organization?
10. What should another LLM not assume?
11. What information is sensitive?
12. What files should update the context after the work is complete?

## 8. Maintenance Recommendation

Review high-change subject files monthly, portfolio and role context quarterly, communication preferences only when Darryl changes them, dated status whenever new evidence is used, stakeholder context after organizational changes, and architecture and pricing before consequential use.
