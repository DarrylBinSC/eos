# CSS Platforms — AI Strategy and Use Cases

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-14  
**Sensitivity:** Internal / Confidential

## 1. Executive Orientation

AI is an executive-level portfolio topic for CSS Platforms.

It should be evaluated on business value, readiness, governance, and measurable outcomes—not novelty.

## 2. Strategic Themes

AI opportunities include:

- Asset and product classification
- Data enrichment
- Document extraction
- Data-quality and anomaly detection
- Ingestion mapping
- Natural-language query
- Customer insight
- Renewal intelligence
- Sales enablement
- Managed Services support
- Support efficiency
- Internal productivity
- Workflow automation

## 3. Most Developed WAM-Oriented Use Cases

### Document-to-Asset or Contract Extraction

Extract structured data from invoices, purchase orders, contracts, asset spreadsheets, PDFs, and images.

Potential value: faster onboarding, reduced manual entry, improved consistency, and better auditability.

### Data-Quality and Anomaly Detection

Scan records for missing fields, invalid relationships, inconsistencies, outliers, expiration risks, and duplicate records.

Potential value: proactive correction, better renewal and lifecycle decisions, and reduced operational review.

### AI-Assisted Mapping and Ingestion

Suggest mappings from customer or supplier data into the WAM model.

Potential value: reduced professional-services effort, faster onboarding, and less dependence on specialized resources.

### Document Intelligence

Allow natural-language questions over asset, contract, license, or product documents.

Potential value: faster information retrieval, better usability, and improved Sales and Managed Services insight.

### Classification and Research

Use AI to research and classify manufacturer parts, software, licenses, warranties, and product attributes.

Potential value: better product data, reduced manual research, and improved MDM and WAM readiness.

## 4. Evaluation Framework

Each AI use case should include:

- Business problem
- User
- Current process
- AI-enabled process
- Data required
- Integration required
- Human-review requirement
- Expected benefit
- Baseline
- Measure
- Cost
- Risk
- Governance
- Pilot design
- Production-readiness criteria

## 5. Readiness Dimensions

- Data quality
- Data rights and privacy
- Technical integration
- Model accuracy
- Explainability
- Human review
- Cost
- Change readiness
- Security
- Enterprise architecture
- Vendor dependency
- Operational ownership

## 6. Key Constraints

- AI output quality depends on source data
- Demonstration performance does not prove production performance
- Human review may remain necessary
- Token or usage cost can vary
- Supplier and customer documents can be inconsistent
- Classification requires confidence and evidence
- Governance must distinguish recommendation from authoritative record
- Integration effort may exceed model-development effort

## 7. Pilot Principle

The strongest initial pilots are bounded, measurable, human-reviewed, based on available data, connected to an existing workflow, and valuable even if full automation is not achieved.

A document-extraction or part-classification pilot may be preferable to a broad AI platform initiative because the outcome and review process are clearer.

## 8. Benefits Standard

Tie AI to reduced manual effort, faster onboarding, improved classification, better renewal intelligence, improved customer insight, more scalable Managed Services, improved support efficiency, and stronger sales enablement.

Avoid generic statements such as “AI will transform the business.”

## 9. Dated Commercial Context

Historical pricing work included an AI Feature Pack model with a base license and usage-based components.

Any pricing, included seats, token structure, or tenant charges must be revalidated before use.

## 10. Instructions for Another LLM

- Separate demonstrated capability from inferred opportunity.
- Do not invent ROI.
- Identify the baseline needed to calculate value.
- Include human review and governance.
- State data dependencies.
- Prefer a measurable pilot over a broad roadmap claim.
- Connect AI to portfolio and service outcomes.
