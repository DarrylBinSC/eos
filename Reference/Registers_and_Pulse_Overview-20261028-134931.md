## Things worth remembering that don't fit a cell

**"Critical" on an Other-Owned item** means: if it went quiet and never came back, something would happen you'd have paid to prevent (a customer sees it, money or contract exposure, or it gates a date). It is a closure-discipline setting, not a priority label; don't use it to mean "important."

**Evidence basis on Watch items** (Direct, Single-source, Repetition, or Inferred) says how grounded the risk is. Mention-frequency alone is never treated as severity.

**Register B/C's practical future scope, now that the lane model exists:** mostly what doesn't come from a governed forum at all, such as a vendor/customer email thread, a 1:1 commitment, or your own choice to delegate something off Register A. Anything that looks like "someone committed to something in a standup or sprint ceremony" is a Pulse candidate first, checked against `_forums.json`.
