## Outside context (Claude research, 2026-10-06; not yet tied to Wesco's use)

aris.com is the commercial ARIS platform, owned by SAG Aris GmbH (the ARIS business formerly part of Software AG). It covers process mining, process modeling, simulation, workflow orchestration, and governance. It is currently marketed as a "process context platform" for enterprise AI and as a "digital twin of the organization": a continuously updated view linking process design to actual execution, so AI agents understand how the business works. A SaaS edition of process mining exists; licensing and deployment details were not on the site.

**Inference to test, not a fact:** if Wesco's Aris runs on this licensed platform, then the product at Wesco is probably an internal capability (process models, mining, AI context) rather than something Wesco builds or sells. The first question is therefore whether this is an internal enterprise program, a platform the team configures, or a customer-facing offer.
