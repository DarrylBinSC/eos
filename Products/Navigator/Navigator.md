## Target-state integration semantics (added 2026-08-27, Darryl direct)

Durable architecture facts, confirmed while reviewing the deck frames. None of these connections exists today.

- **NavWork is the single integration point on the Navigator side.** entroCIM reaches **NavIntel through NavWork**, not directly. One interface to build, govern and support; the front end stays a front end.
- entroCIM and NavWork exchange both ways: condition becomes work over the API, asset and work status return.
- **CDA connects to both platforms and both ways:** device health and evidence into entroCIM, device risk into NavWork as work.
- **CDA's coverage includes metering and building automation**, not only the connected device estate of cameras, access control and network gear.
- **WAM connects to both:** warranty, entitlement and install-base context to NavWork, asset and lifecycle context to entroCIM.

**Interim-state term, worth keeping:** the entroCIM to Ascent Ops link before June 2027 is a **swivel-chair**. The person is the integration. Naming it makes the end state's value measurable rather than rhetorical, since removing the swivel-chair is the thing the integration actually does.


## DJ Vaughn's published roadmap (added 2026-08-28)

Source: `Deliverables/Navigator/2026-09-DJ_Navigator_Platform_Current_State_MVP1_3_Year_Roadmap.pptx`, Navigator Platform executive review, September 2026, twelve slides. **These are the governing dates. Darryl's ruling 2026-08-28: use DJ's published dates throughout so the two decks do not confuse anyone; update over time as work progresses.**

**Three-year executive view**

| Year | Period | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | Q3 2027 to Q2 2028 | Deploy and Connect. Deploy and stabilize NavWork; maintain Navigator Intelligence; establish tenant and client access; **begin entroCIM connectivity** |
| Year 2 | Q3 2028 to Q2 2029 | Expand Operations. Vendor and customer capabilities; **facility data and field activity**; rounds, inspections and contracts; inventory and workforce functions |
| Year 3 | Q3 2029 to Q2 2030 | Scale and Extend. Planning and administrative tools; automation and scorecards; safety, audit and failure analysis; additional support functions |

**The two entroCIM quarters, and they are the ones to hold:**

- **Q4 2027, "Operate after deployment":** Integration support, entroCIM. Also tenant application, client user management, service and access requests.
- **Q2 2028, "Begin facility connectivity":** Integration expansion, **meters, CM points, MOP data point gathering**.

**Consequence for the interim state.** The interim entroCIM connection carries roughly eighteen months, not six. That makes it considerably more valuable than a short bridge, and it makes the swivel-chair period long enough to be worth removing.

**MVP1 scope (DJ slides 5 to 7), for context on the December 2026 date.** Operating records: organizations, users, assets and locations, manufacturers and classifications, work orders, service and access requests, work templates and routes, purchase requests and orders, invoices and accounting, contracts, vendors and catalog. Record experience: list pages, filters, saved queries, record creation and maintenance, status change, roles, security groups, permissions, audit and workflow history. Remaining: workflow designer and execution, email functions, landing pages by role, scheduled record generation, reports and KPIs, and Navigator/Maximo ETL migration covering asset, route, vendor, work, purchasing, invoice, contract, template and catalog data plus security, API and service transition.

**Current state per DJ (slide 3), which closes the gap the record previously carried:** Navigator Intelligence is live for managed services; Maximo Today is the current operational system of record covering assets, locations and equipment history, work orders, service and access requests, purchasing, invoices, contracts and vendors, and is the source for migration into NavWork; NavWork in development has front-end and back-end foundations established, with security, records and common functions underway, and deployment designed to preserve live operations.
