# O-Insights Core (CTS) — vendor under evaluation
As of: 2026-10-26. Vendor file, not a Wesco product; not in the Products tab roster. Read when O-Insights, CTS, Genie AI, or a Milestone-connected analytics platform comes up.

## What it is
A building operations and analytics platform ("O-Insights Core") from a company the vendor's own material names only as CTS (ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified). Modules: portfolio dashboards with GIS map, reporting, FDD with a visual low-code rule builder, alarm manager with multi-level escalation (email, SMS, WhatsApp), 3D visualization (a scene viewer and editor fed by converted model files; no BIM, IFC, COBie or CAD claimed; formats unknown), historical playback to video, IP camera video in the interface. Roadmap: Asset Manager (CMMS), Predictive Maintenance ("Gyan AI/ML"), Genie AI (conversational and agentic assistant). Windows services or Linux K3s; cloud-hosted and white-label available. Pre-release at the demo (mid-July 2026 readiness target); MQTT in the initial release, BACnet, OPC UA and SNMP targeted end of September 2026; Modbus not listed.
