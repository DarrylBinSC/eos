### Investment Description (no change from original)

For facility managers, building operators, enterprise real estate teams, and industrial organizations who need to optimize complex facilities and connected infrastructure but lack a unified view of operational data, the entroCIM platform is a centralized intelligent facilities management solution that delivers real-time visibility, automation, analytics, and energy optimization to improve efficiency and reduce costs. Unlike disconnected monitoring and management tools, our solution provides a scalable, integrated platform that enables proactive decision-making and continuous operational improvement across facilities worldwide.

### Risk of NOT Doing (FINAL, Darryl's shortened version, agreed 2026-07-24)

Without continued investment in entroCIM, Wesco risks losing competitive differentiation, recurring revenue opportunities, and the ability to scale its smart building and operational intelligence offerings. It would also reduce Wesco's control of the customer relationship and installed-device data while increasing the cost and time required to rebuild these capabilities in the future.

**Loss of Competitive Differentiation**
entroCIM gives Wesco a proprietary smart facilities platform and greater control of the customer experience, customer relationship, and operational data. Without it, Wesco would become more dependent on third-party platforms and less differentiated from traditional distributors and point-solution providers.

**Missed Recurring Revenue Growth**
Reducing or discontinuing investment would limit Wesco's ability to expand software, managed services, and other recurring revenue streams tied to intelligent buildings and operational technology.

**Reduced Market Access and Deployment Flexibility**
entroCIM is designed to operate in the cloud, in a customer-managed environment, or on compact hardware at the customer site. This flexibility supports government, regulated, and security-sensitive customers that single-deployment competitors may be unable to serve. Without continued investment, Wesco would forfeit this market reach and weaken the platform foundation required to scale connected facilities, energy management, and operational intelligence solutions.

**Lost Value from Wesco's Installed Device Base**
Wesco sells and distributes a growing portfolio of connected and converged devices. entroCIM enables Wesco to connect to, monitor, and support those devices after the sale. Without this capability, Wesco's relationship may end at shipment, leaving associated service, renewal, and pull-through revenue unrealized or available to competitors.

**Fragmentation Across the Digital Services Portfolio**
entroCIM provides a common operational foundation for Intelligent Facility Management Services and other connected, data-driven offerings. Shared device connectivity, data, and operational intelligence reduce duplication and increase the combined value of the portfolio. Without a common platform, individual offerings would require separate capabilities, increasing delivery costs and limiting their collective value.

**Higher Cost and Delayed Launch of Future Services**
Future recurring services will depend on a trusted, ongoing connection to customers' installed devices. Deferring investment in entroCIM would increase the cost, complexity, and time required to launch each subsequent offering, delaying future revenue and creating additional modernization expense.

### The strategic tension worth deciding deliberately

The audience-calibration rule (sustain, do not expand) and the scoring sheet pull in opposite directions. Sustaining language minimizes scrutiny; scoring drives portfolio funding priority. Scoring entroCIM conservatively across the board is consistent with the narrative but produces a low relative rank in a constrained cycle, and rank is what allocates money.

Recommended resolution: keep the **narrative** sustaining, and score honestly rather than modestly on the two criteria where entroCIM is genuinely strong and the claim is verifiable (Customer Success, and Financial Benefits once the proforma supports it). Correct the three internally inconsistent ratings and drop Compliance. A scoring sheet that is internally consistent and defensible field by field survives review; one with a High compliance claim and three contradictions does not, regardless of how well the narrative reads.
