## Superseded text (2026-07-28) — retained for traceability, do not use

Risk of NOT Doing

Without investment in Connected Device Assurance, the connected devices Wesco sells are left unmanaged after the sale. The recurring service revenue, refresh demand, and ongoing customer relationship attached to that installed base go unrealized or are captured by specialist platform providers, while the cost of entering the market later continues to rise.

Unrealized Recurring Revenue from Devices Already Sold
Cameras, access control, and network devices require firmware, configuration, health, and security upkeep for their entire operating life. Wesco sells the devices but does not participate in that recurring need today. Without this service, the subscription revenue attached to the installed base is left to others or goes unserved.

Lost Refresh and Pull-Through Demand
An assurance service continuously identifies end-of-support conditions, repeated failures, and replacement candidates from its own operating data and delivers them to Sales as qualified opportunities. Without it, refresh demand stays invisible until a competitor finds it first.

Ceding the Category to Specialist Providers
Third-party device-management platforms are already selling recurring device-security and management services into the customer segments Wesco serves. Each year of delay strengthens their position, raises the cost of eventual entry, and narrows the differentiation available to Wesco.

Stranded Return on Platform Investment Already Made
The service is assembled from capabilities Wesco has already funded: entroCIM and its containerization work, WAM lifecycle data, and secure remote connectivity. A focused investment converts these into a revenue-bearing service. Not proceeding strands that reuse and leaves the containerization investment serving a single platform.

Fragmentation of the Connected Services Portfolio
Connected Device Assurance supplies device condition, evidence, and field work to Intelligent Facility Management Services and the broader family of connected offerings, and its operating data strengthens asset lifecycle and field service delivery. Without it, each offering must source device connection and device intelligence separately, raising delivery costs across the portfolio.

Growing Customer Exposure Without a Wesco Answer
Unmanaged device firmware and configuration is an expanding cyber and compliance liability for customers, and demand for accountable remediation is rising across OT and IoT environments. Without an offer, Wesco's customers meet that demand elsewhere, and the trusted device relationship that future services depend on is built by someone else.
