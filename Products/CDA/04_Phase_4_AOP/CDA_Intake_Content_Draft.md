## Long-form reference versions (for the business case, not the intake slide)

## Business Problem | Opportunity Statement

Connected devices that Wesco sells, including cameras, access control, and network equipment, are deployed and then left under-managed. Firmware goes out of date, device health is unknown until failure, and cyber posture degrades device by device with no accountable owner. Customers carry growing cyber and compliance exposure they are not equipped to manage, and Wesco's relationship with each device effectively ends at shipment.

Connected Device Assurance Services is a recurring managed service that takes ownership of that risk end to end: helping the customer establish the compliance framework their device governance depends on, identifying and monitoring device posture, managing and executing firmware and configuration upgrades under customer approval, and escalating to field service when remote resolution is not possible. It is delivered through Wesco service tools built on entroCIM with a partner sensor for discovery and vulnerability context, deployed on a containerized service node or from the Wesco cloud. Wesco Managed Services owns the service level, with Wesco TSS professional services delivering the compliance framework design and customer onboarding.

Industry guidance supports the need. CISA's 2025 OT asset inventory guidance, issued jointly with eight other agencies, establishes device inventory as a foundational requirement, and NIST IR 8259A defines the device capabilities that make a device securable. An internal white paper commissioned by SPARC/VOI and authored by Wesco documents that enterprise members cannot operationalize this alone and identifies partner-led managed delivery as the fastest path to maturity.

The service creates recurring revenue from the installed base, generates qualified refresh and replacement demand for Sales, and serves as the lead-in service for Intelligent Asset Management Services and entroCIM-enabled services, since the secure connection and verified device inventory it establishes are what those services need to begin.
