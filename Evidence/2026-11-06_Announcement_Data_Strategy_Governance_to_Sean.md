# Leadership announcement: Data Strategy & Governance team to Sean McCloskey (received 2026-11-06)

Source: pasted by Darryl, 2026-11-06. Sender not stated in the pasted text; treat the author as unconfirmed (reads as from Akash Khurana or above, since it moves a Digital IT team under Sean while confirming his reporting line to Akash). Verbatim below.

---

As we continue preparing for our early 2027 DDP deployments, it is important that we strengthen our focus on master data management, data governance and data strategy to support execution at scale. To help drive these priorities and ensure alignment across our digital and business initiatives, we are making a leadership change within the Data Strategy & Governance team.

Effective today, Sean McCloskey will continue to report to Akash Khurana and will assume leadership of the Data Strategy & Governance team within Digital IT in addition to his current responsibilities. The team will report to Sean, and his expanded scope will include master data management (MDM), data governance and data strategy across key enterprise data domains, including product, supplier and customer data. This includes establishing the governance, standards, ownership and data management practices needed to improve data quality, consistency and availability across these domains.

Sean brings the business and technical experience needed to lead this work. His immediate priority will be to focus the team on the MDM deliverables required for our early 2027 DDP deployments. He will also partner closely with the Data Office to strengthen enterprise MDM processes, data quality and governance across these critical data domains as we scale deployment.

Sean will also partner with Sara Nixon in the execution of our master data strategy, combining EPMO discipline, governance, tooling and support to accelerate scaling DDP. This ensures our data initiatives remain tightly aligned with the broader EPMO framework as we deploy DDP broadly. Sara will continue reporting to Rob Hayston with her current responsibilities, while actively supporting the execution and coordination of our data priorities.

As part of this transition, Darren Taylor is no longer with Wesco. We thank Darren for his contributions and wish him all the best in his future endeavors.

Please join me in supporting Sean, the Data Strategy & Governance team and Sara as they advance this important work.
