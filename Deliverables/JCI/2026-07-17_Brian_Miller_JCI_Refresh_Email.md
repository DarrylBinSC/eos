**To:** Brian Miller
**Subject:** JCI OpenBlue Airwall MSP Agreement — restarting legal review; meeting next week

Brian,

I want to get the JCI Airwall MSP agreement moving again. Legal review has been idle since early June, and two things have changed since we last connected:

1. We met with JCI's account team on July 15. They verbally confirmed the MSP operating model assumes Wesco continues to host and manage the Airwall Conductor in our Azure environment, with JCI adding a read-only agent for monthly license metering. That is exactly the position we want — but the current draft doesn't expressly grant it, so we need it reflected in the agreement language.

2. We completed a review of the draft that surfaced additional terms needing a legal position beyond the five items we were tracking: indemnity scope, liability cap carve-outs, JCI unilateral change rights, non-cancelable orders, price-increase notice, customer flow-down terms, and a potential competing-offerings restriction.

Can you hold 45–60 minutes next week? Proposed agenda:

1. **Hosting rights (priority).** Redline to expressly permit Wesco to host and manage the Conductor and MSP infrastructure in Wesco Azure, with JCI access limited to API-based license monitoring plus support access we authorize. Decide whether the agreement's existing "Non-Hosted JCI Offerings" definition gets us there or new language is needed — and whether it belongs in the agreement body or the Order exhibit.

2. **Status of the five open items.** Hosting rights, API/license-monitoring data scope, customer agreement requirement, final MSP pricing, and a named JCI counsel contact for you.

3. **New terms from the draft review.** Indemnity, liability carve-outs, unilateral changes, non-cancelable orders, price-increase notice — where we accept, counter, or need business input from me.

4. **Customer-contracting terms.** Flow-down of the JCI Customer Agreement and the prohibition on termination for convenience, assessed against our standard customer agreements. In our hosted model, customers never interact with Airwall — the customer-terms structure needs to work with that.

5. **Next steps and dates.** Target markup schedule and what we need from JCI to get there.

Useful context: the Conductor is already running in our Azure environment, and we're routing the JCI license-monitoring API connection through the Architecture Review Board. Hosting is our operating reality — the agreement needs to catch up to it. We're also drafting the hosting/support responsibility split (patching, backup, DR, SLA, support access) as a proposed exhibit; I'll share it ahead of the meeting if it's ready.

I'll send an invite — let me know which day works best.

Darryl
