## 4. Six framing points, and they matter commercially

1. **Nothing is integrated yet.** entroCIM, WAM, Navigator and CDA are designed to connect. No production data flows between any two of them today. entroCIM integration support in Navigator begins Q4 2027 on DJ Vaughn's published roadmap; facility connectivity lands Q2 2028. Say "planned" every time.
2. **CDA is not an entroCIM feature.** It uses entroCIM's underlying platform, tools and engineering team, and it is sold as a managed service. Describing it as an entroCIM add-on misprices it and confuses the buyer.
3. **WAM attainment and the WAM roadmap are two different stories.** The revenue is coming from supply chain programs. The roadmap investment is in the license capability. A reviewer can reasonably ask what the license capability has produced commercially, and that answer is still being built. Do not present the $16.7M as evidence that the license roadmap is converting.
4. **entroCIM's constraint is the commercial system, not the product.** Ten years in market with an established install base, materially behind a $6.6M software target. The open work is conversion, packaging and sales motion. That is commercialization work, which is why this is the highest-value place for the new role to engage.
5. **Two named sales-lead gaps.** WAM has no single sales lead. Navigator has no CSS sales motion at all, and its metrics are produced inside Ascent's operation, which Darryl does not control.
6. **Benefits discipline.** Distinguish planned from realized throughout. The WAM benefits-underreporting history with John Engel and Rob Hasting is why this portfolio holds that line explicitly.
