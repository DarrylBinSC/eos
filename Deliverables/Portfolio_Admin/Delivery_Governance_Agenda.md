# Deployment Governance Readout: standing agenda

Weekly, 45 minutes. Chad Steger and Syed Jamshed Zaidi report to Darryl Benson on the health of the deployment business. Created 2026-10-16, revised the same day to a readout format. Owner: Darryl. The agenda through "Operating rules" can be shared with Chad and Syed as written.

Purpose: Chad and Syed give Darryl an executive readout on how the deployment business is performing, where it is blocked, and whether the project process is working. Darryl's role is to ask questions, remove obstacles outside the team's reach, and make the decisions that are his. Execution detail stays with the team.

## Agenda

1. **Headline (5 min).** Chad and Syed open with the state of the deployment business in a few sentences: green, amber or red overall, and the one thing Darryl should know this week. Then customers: any account whose confidence or satisfaction has moved, Dell first.
2. **Delivery status (10 min).** Active deployments and migrations by account: on plan, at risk, or slipped, with the reason for anything not on plan. Commitments made to customers in the past week. Dates that moved and whether the customer knows.
3. **Roadblocks (10 min).** What is blocked, for how long, and who holds the key. Split into two lists: blockers the team can clear itself (what is the plan and the date) and blockers that need Darryl (another team, a vendor, a customer executive, money or contract). Only the second list gets discussed in depth.
4. **Process health (10 min).** How the project process is working, not the individual issues. What the process caught and handled on its own this week. What reached this meeting that should have been handled earlier, and why. Where the process is being bypassed or is slowing the team down. What changed in the process since last week.
5. **People and capacity (5 min).** Load across the team, who is stretched, where knowledge sits with one person and what the backup is, planned absences and departures that touch a customer or a date.
6. **Asks of Darryl (5 min).** What Chad and Syed need: a decision, resources, air cover, a conversation with a customer or a peer, an escalation upward. Each ask leaves with an answer or a date for one.

## Operating rules

- Chad and Syed present; Darryl asks questions. Slides are optional; a verbal readout against this agenda is enough.
- The test for every item raised: does Darryl need to decide, intervene, or be able to speak to it if asked by Sean or a customer? If not, it stays with the team.
- Ticket, site and configuration detail moves to the Darryl and Chad 1:1 or the Daily Scrum.
- Every ask and every outside blocker leaves with an owner, a next action and a date.
- Bad news early is the expectation. A date that moved is reported the week it moved, not when it becomes visible.
- Quarterly, section 4 becomes a full process review: intake, triage, escalation path, closeout, and what the process is missing.

## Darryl's preparation (not shared)

- Before the meeting, read the Delivery track in the Pulse: calls, stuck items, new items, quiet items, People and Knowledge. Use it to form questions, not to lead the meeting. If the readout misses something the Pulse shows, ask about it in the section where it belongs; a gap between the two is itself a process-health finding for section 4.
- During the meeting, record outcomes in the Pulse (Escalate, Leave with team, Add a note) and any accepted ask in Register A.
- Afterwards, note in the Pulse anything the readout contradicted so the evidence base stays honest.
