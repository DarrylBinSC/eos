## Message 1 to David Blondel and Akansh Gangil (WAM)

Send: Teams, group chat with both.

---

David, Akansh,

I am putting a standard structure on the weekly WAM leadership sync, starting the week of September 8.

The main change is a rolling agenda register that I will maintain and publish 24 hours before each session. Every open item sits on it with one named owner, a next step and a date, and it carries forward week to week until it is closed with a recorded resolution. Nothing comes off because it went quiet. Anything sitting four weeks or more without movement gets disposed of in the session: escalated, re-dated with a reason, reassigned, or killed.

The session itself runs decisions first, no more than three, then a walk of the whole register, then anything new, then ten minutes of open discussion, then a readback of every decision and new item with an owner and a date. David, you chair it. Status that does not need a decision goes in writing beforehand rather than being read out. Send me your items 48 hours ahead.

We are also turning transcription on. Not recording, just the transcript, so a summary with the decisions and actions can go into the chat within a day. David, edit that summary before it posts. The automatic notes get attribution wrong often enough to matter.

The ten minutes of open discussion is real and I am not trying to remove it. Some of the most useful things that have come out of that meeting came out of the unstructured part. What I am removing is the version where we spend fifty minutes there and never reach the decisions.

The reason is the truncated part number issue and the EUDM sample orders. Both have been raised in that room more than once, both are still without a named owner, and neither has a date. That is what an unstructured meeting costs. I will send the first register on Friday.

Darryl

## If someone pushes back on transcription

In this order:

1. It is a transcript, not a recording. Nobody is on video and nothing is rewatchable.
2. Most of the other forums in this portfolio are already transcribed. These two are the exception, and they are the ones carrying the decisions.
3. The open discussion block can run with transcription paused. Make the offer before anyone has to ask for it.

If the objection persists, the fallback is no transcription and a chair-written five-line summary posted to the chat within 24 hours. The written record is the requirement. Transcription is the cheapest way to get it, not the only way. The register does not depend on it either way.
