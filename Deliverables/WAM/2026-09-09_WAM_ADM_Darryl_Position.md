**Produced:** 2026-09-09

**This file does not go to Sean and is not in the deck.** It is Part 9 of the feasibility work, deliberately held outside the pack. Sean asked to present the evidence and hold the decision discussion in his own meeting, so the pack reaches no verdict. This is what Darryl says if he is asked directly.

### The recommendation, if asked

Option C, in a specific form: product ownership and backlog control move to Digital Products explicitly, development stays where it is, and the move is conditioned on named owners and dated commitments for W1, W2 and the MDM license-term item. Not option B, and not option D.

The reasoning is that the evidence supports one conclusion strongly and the rest weakly. Twelve of twenty-one data dependencies, and every dependency gating a customer-facing use case, sit outside Digital Products. A development transfer changes none of them. So option B pays a transition cost, including a live risk to the September release and the Violet Moyer commitment, in exchange for a change that does not move the binding constraint. Option D forfeits an unquantified set of internal commitments to save $150,480 of FY27 expense, which is not a trade anyone would make with the numbers stated plainly. Option A leaves whatever prompted the question unaddressed.

Option C is the only one that puts prioritization where the accountability already sits without paying for a move, and the condition attached to it is the thing that actually changes the outcome.

### What Darryl needs from Sean

Cross-functional weight on the three defects and the MDM license-term item. This is the ask that only a VP can satisfy, and it is worth more than any answer to the ownership question. The escalation route available at Darryl's level has been used and has not produced an owner.

An answer to what prompted the transfer question. Without it, the pack is answering a question Darryl inferred rather than the one Sean asked.

A decision on the license-versus-supply-chain motion, open since 2026-08-05 with no decision taken. Every feasibility rating in Part 4 assumes the license capability is still the target. If it is not, the ratings are answering the wrong question and the whole roadmap should follow the demand instead.

### Questions to expect, and the answers

**"Why does none of this have a number?"** Because no one has asked for one, and that is Darryl's own gap rather than anyone else's. Every figure in Tier 1 of the sequenced path is obtainable inside Digital Products in a week. Answer it that way, own it, and give the date. Do not explain why measurement is difficult.

**"If the constraints are all external, what have you been doing?"** The record answers this without defensiveness: the notification suite in production, the enrichment MVP delivered against a live requirement in two days, the complex-build architecture confirmed and the capture point made explicit in warehouse standard work, the IDP path taken through architecture approval. What has not been done is measurement, and the escalations that would produce owners for the three defects have been made and have not worked. That second half is the ask.

**"Should we just stop?"** The honest answer is that the record cannot support a stop decision today, because whether the supply chain programs that earn the revenue depend on ADM is not established. That is G7, it is answerable in a week, and Darryl should offer to answer it rather than argue against the option.

**"Is Akansh a single point of failure?"** Yes, and it is documented, reconfirmed on 2026-09-09, and unaddressed under every option including the status quo. Do not defend it. It is a real finding and offering it unprompted is more credible than conceding it under questioning.

### What to hold back

Do not offer the recommendation unless asked. Sean said he wants to present the evidence and hold the discussion in his meeting; leading with a recommendation takes that away from him and invites the reading that the pack was built to reach a predetermined answer. If the room stalls, the go or no-go page in 6.5 is the instrument for restarting it, not Darryl's opinion.

Do not raise the Strev funding attribution question in this meeting unless Sean opens it. It is a real decision and it is a different one, and mixing a funding-attribution question into an ownership discussion invites the answer that the money should wait for the ownership decision.
