# entroCIM Data Center Value Proposition: Plan

Prepared October 7, 2026, revised the same day. Deliverable due to Sean McCloskey the week of October 19.

::: {custom-style="Bottom Line"}
**Bottom line:** the deck shows where entroCIM can win in the data center markets Wesco is already going after, and what it takes to get entroCIM moving in those spaces. The first step is agreeing the purpose and output with Nate before anything is built.
:::

## Purpose

Show where we can win, and help drive the business to get entroCIM going in those spaces. The deck answers Sean's question about entroCIM's real ability to move the needle in data centers by tying it to Wesco's own data center go-to-market rather than to a separate entroCIM pitch.

## What the deck covers

1. **Alignment with Wesco's data center go-to-market.** The segments Wesco is pursuing, and where entroCIM fits in each.
2. **How we bring value.** What entroCIM does for a data center operator, stated as current capability, with planned capability shown separately.
3. **Aligned services.** The Wesco services that make entroCIM sellable and deliverable in each segment.
4. **Portfolio connections.** How entroCIM, Ascent Navigator and CDA connect in a data center. This reuses the "Three platforms stronger" graphic from the CDA deck, recast for the data center context, with current and planned capability kept separate.
5. **Where we win and what is needed.** Priority segments, target accounts, and the support required from Sales and leadership.

## Wesco's data center business

Data center sales were about $1.4 billion in Q1 2026, roughly 24 percent of Wesco's total sales, growing about 70 percent year over year across hyperscale, multi-tenant and enterprise customers. The Data Center Solutions group, formed from the Rahi Systems acquisition, is focused on hyperscale and multi-tenant customers.

## Segment priority

| Priority | Segment | Why |
|--|------------|--------------|
| 1 | Enterprise-owned data centers | Closest to entroCIM's current strengths and to Wesco's Global Enterprise accounts |
| 2 | Multi-tenant and colocation | A priority segment for Wesco's Data Center Solutions group; operators run many sites and buy for operational efficiency |
| 3 | Hyperscale | Likely out of reach for entroCIM today; operators build their own tooling. Covered last, for alignment |

::: {custom-style="Source Note"}
**Note:** the priority order is my working view. Wesco's data center volume is weighted toward hyperscale and multi-tenant, so the deck needs to say plainly why entroCIM leads with enterprise and how it still supports the larger data center business.
:::
