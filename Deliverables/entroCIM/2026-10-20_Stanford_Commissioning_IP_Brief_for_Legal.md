## What the documents say

**Document 1 (CODA proposal, 2021-10-20).** Hepta Systems proposes "to develop and deliver development of custom commissioning/functional testing programming within EntroCIM." Scope: test actions and test verifications combined into tests; tests directed at targets by filter; immediate or scheduled execution; a test engine that manages execution and stores results; summary and detailed reporting, viewable online and exportable to PDF; a Commissioning Test App (Test Management View, Test Instance Management View) and a Commissioning Report App (Report Builder, Report Viewer); documentation. Deliverable: "transmitted via a .pod for the EntroCIM installations," example functions in plaintext Axon, documentation on "the IoT Warez support interface." Exclusions include: "The EntroCIM Yearly Software Maintenance Fee will be adjusted due to the addition of the Commission Application." Payment: $25,000 initial invoice, remainder progress billed. Appendices A and B are Newcomb & Boyd's sequence of operations and FDD rules for the site.

**Document 3 (Stanford quote, 2022-01-13).** The Scope of Work, Interface and Deliverable sections are the same text as document 1, apart from line breaks and one phrase ("in EntroCIM" in document 1; "in Axon (native SkySpark scripting language)" in document 3). Differences: the examples are Stanford's Gates FTP documents dated 2021-08-01 rather than the CODA appendices; document 3 adds development milestones (system design 2 to 4 days; test engine 2 weeks; system views 1 to 2 weeks; Stanford Gates tests 2 weeks; final testing and documentation 1 to 2 weeks), an estimated 225 to 325 hours at $200 per hour, and payment of $25,000 up front with the balance progress billed. No IP, ownership, license or governing-terms language. PDF metadata: created 2021-10-12, modified 2022-01-13.

**Document 4 (PO, 2022-01-31).** Line item: "WAREZ LLC - Quote dated 1/13/22 develop and delivery of Functional Testing Programming within EntroCIM. Scope Of Work = development of 'test action's and 'test verification's in Axon (nativeSkySpark scripting language). Start Date = 31-Jan-2022. End Date = 30-Apr-2022." Terms block: "This Purchase Contract may be accepted only on the terms set forth herein. The complete Terms and Conditions can be found at [document 7 URL]. Terms in any acceptance by Seller which are in addition hereto or not identical with the terms hereof will not become a part of any Purchase Contract unless Buyer specifically and expressly agrees in writing that such other terms are accepted. By accepting this Purchase Contract or any part hereon, Seller agrees to and accepts all the provisions of the Purchase Contract."

**Document 5 (Stanford quote, 2023-10-17).** "Within entroCIM's Commissioning toolsets, as previously developed for the Gates Building, we will provide custom commissioning scripts" for named equipment (VAV, CHW, heating hot water, fan coil units, AHU1 to 3, DHW), 139 tests at $250 each, AHU2 "tbd." Assumptions include "Work to be completed on customer-owned software with remote access via clustering" and a "customer-owned and provided entroCIM instance." Exclusions: "This price does not include functional/software development updates to the Commissioning toolset itself." No IP, ownership or license language.

**Document 6 (PO, 2023-11-13).** Line item: "ENTROCIM - LBRE Commissioning Quote dated 10/17/23. Complete Test and Scripting development for Software platform (EntroCIM). Scope Of Work = Providing custom commissioning scripts for Entrocim software platform. Start Date = 06-Nov-2023. End Date = 31-Mar-2024. Project Manager = Frank Durso. Proposal Attached (Y/N) = Y." Same terms block as document 4.

**Document 7 (Stanford terms, current version).** Article 18.2: Stanford exclusively owns Work Product, Technology and related IP Rights created, conceived or reduced to practice while performing Services, with a present assignment by the contractor. Article 18.4: Work Product and Technology exclude IP created, owned or licensed by the contractor before the PO date or developed independently of it, including tools, methodologies and software components; the contractor retains those; where such Excluded Materials are necessary to use the Work Product, the contractor grants Stanford a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, irrevocable, non-exclusive, sublicensable license to the extent necessary for Stanford's intended use. Article 18.5: the contractor may use Work Product produced under the agreement only if Stanford has made it publicly available or has approved the use in writing. The page states the terms may be updated by Stanford from time to time; we have not obtained the versions in force on 2022-01-31 or 2023-11-13.
