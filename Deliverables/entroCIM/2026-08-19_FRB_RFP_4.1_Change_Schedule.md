# Change 3
**Location:** Section 4.1, immediately beneath the Network Segment table.

**Action:** Add as a new note.

> **Note on OT protocol security.** Field-level protocols such as BACnet/IP and Modbus/TCP do not provide native encryption or authentication. This is a characteristic of the protocols themselves rather than of any single system. The architecture therefore confines these protocols to dedicated, segmented OT VLANs with routing controlled at the local firewall, restricts communication to the specific device and port pairs required, and encrypts all traffic leaving the OT segments toward users, APIs, and enterprise systems. IoT device traffic uses MQTT over TLS on port 8883 or HTTPS on port 443 where the devices support it.

**Why:** The table displays cleartext protocols while Section 3.1 emphasizes encryption. A Federal Reserve security reviewer will notice. Naming the limitation and the compensating control converts it from a finding into evidence of a considered design. It is much better to say this first than to answer it under questioning.

**Location:** Section 4.1, end of the paragraph beginning "All device communications remain on the customer LAN."

**Action:** Add one sentence. **Gated on confirmation from Ron Snyder.**

**If no outbound connectivity is required, add:**
> entroCIM requires no outbound internet connectivity for normal operation. Software updates and licence management are performed through Owner-approved channels under Owner change control.

**If outbound connectivity is required for licensing, activation, or updates, add instead:**

Do not write either version until Ron confirms which is true, and note that the licensed analytics platform beneath entroCIM is part of that question, not just entroCIM itself.
