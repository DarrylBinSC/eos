## What needs your attention

**Enterprise pricing is the material exception.** At the sponsorship review, Peter Ferrell said the per-site packages work as a land offer but enterprise rollouts will be negotiated, and that Kroger will not pay "$10 a device times 60,000." That is close to what the plan charges: Kroger's 429 sites average about 140 devices against the 200 the Large fee assumes, so the plan prices Kroger at about $9.35 per device per month. Sridhar Loke added that the fees read as cost-plus. The only customer price signal so far is Kroger asking Bob Caprise for a device manager at about $15 per camera, and whether that is one-time or annual is not confirmed.

::: {custom-style="Risk"}
**Concentration risk:** Kroger is 64 percent of 2031 revenue, and Kroger with Cisco is 77 percent of the run rate. Kroger is engaged; nothing is contracted. The Cisco-side owner has not been identified. The other three anchors are placeholders by vertical.
:::

**Small sites need a different delivery pattern.** Banks and branch estates with fewer than ten cameras a site cannot justify an appliance each. The likely answer is serving branches from a regional collector, the pattern entroCIM already uses at Dell. That is an architecture change as well as a pricing one.

**Service delivery has to be built.** The plan needs 18 service-delivery heads by 2031, all new hires, with the first in seat mid-2027. TSS has not yet reviewed the heads or hours, and Sridhar raised whether Wesco can sell managed services at this scale.

## For your awareness

- **Scope:** the sponsorship review produced more use cases than CDA carries at launch. I am writing the launch definition and a one-page seller comparison before sales conversations set expectations.
