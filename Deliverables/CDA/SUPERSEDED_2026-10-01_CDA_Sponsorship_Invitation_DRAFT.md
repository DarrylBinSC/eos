**Subject:** Connected Device Assurance: business case review and sponsorship

Bill, Bob, Jay, Peter,

My team has built Connected Device Assurance (CDA) into a full business case. Bob's input on the financials and anchor accounts shaped much of it, and I'd like to walk the four of you through where it landed.

CDA is a managed service that keeps the connected devices Wesco sells, starting with cameras, inventoried, patched and secure for one annual fee per site. Wesco owns the hardware, so the customer commits no capital. Sellers are paid commission on three years of contract value at signing.

What I'm asking for:

- **A business sponsor.** Bill, I'd like you to sponsor CDA: review the case, help name the anchor accounts, and advocate for it inside sales leadership. No quota or headcount comes with the role.
- **Anchor accounts.** Three to five named accounts across retail, pharma and automotive, drawn from our largest customers. Kroger is engaged; I'd like your help naming the rest.

Agenda:

- The problem and the service (10 min)
- How it sells and what a seller earns (10 min)
- The economics, in brief (5 min)
- Sponsorship, anchor accounts and next steps (20 min)

I'll send the deck ahead of the meeting. If this slot doesn't work, let me know and I'll move it.

Thanks,
Darryl

## Notes for Darryl (not for sending)

- **Why the heads-up note.** Asking Bill to sponsor in front of three peers puts him on the spot. A short direct note first lets him arrive with his answer formed, which makes the group meeting a confirmation rather than a cold ask.
- **What to send as the pre-read.** Remove slide 19 (Slides by audience); it names what each audience, including Bill, is shown. Slide 16 (Fee build) carries component costs; fine for these four inside Wesco, but keep it out of anything forwarded to customers.
- **Sean stays out of the group invite.** Naming Sean's condition in front of Bill's peers reads as pressure on Bill. It sits in the private note instead, where it gives him the reason without an audience.
- **Two placeholders to fill** in the note to Bill: the time the sponsor role takes, and two times you are free.
- **Roles not on file.** Jay Maes's and Peter Ferrell's roles are not in the EOS, so the invite asks the group, not them individually. If either owns an account lane or a region, one line naming what you need from them would sharpen the ask.
