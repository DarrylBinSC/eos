## Group invite

**To:** Bill Muczko, Bob Caprise, Jay Maes, Peter Ferrell
**Subject:** Connected Device Assurance: business case review and sponsorship
**Length:** 45 minutes

Bill, Bob, Jay, Peter,

My team and I have built Connected Device Assurance (CDA) into a full business case. Bob's input on the financials and anchor accounts shaped much of it, and I'd like to walk the four of you through where it landed.

CDA is a managed service that keeps the connected devices Wesco sells, starting with cameras, inventoried, patched, and secure for one annual fee per site. Wesco owns the hardware, so the customer commits no capital. Sellers are paid commission on three years of contract value at signing.

What I'm asking for:

- A business sponsor: Bill, I'd like you to sponsor CDA: review the case, help name the anchor accounts, and advocate for it inside sales leadership. No quota or headcount comes with the role through the build phase.
- Anchor accounts: Three to five named accounts across retail, pharma, and automotive, drawn from our largest customers. Kroger has been mentioned by Bill. I'd like your help naming the rest.

Agenda:

- The problem and the service (10 min)
- How it sells and what a seller earns (10 min)
- The economics, in brief (5 min)
- Sponsorship, anchor accounts and next steps (20 min)

This will all be within a slide deck I'll run you through.

Regards,
Darryl
