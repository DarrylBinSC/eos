# CDA: Status and FY27 Funding Direction

Prepared for Sean McCloskey by Darryl Benson, 2026-10-06.

::: {custom-style="Bottom Line"}
**Bottom line:** CDA has a business sponsor, and the business case review agreed to continue the work. I need your view on FY27: is CDA a project I can get funded, or should I fold the capability into the entroCIM roadmap?
:::

## Status

The business case review on 2026-10-02 brought together Bill Muczko, Bob Caprise, Peter Ferrell, David Fisher and Sridhar Loke. The group accepted the problem CDA solves: connected devices drift out of support, license and cyber compliance after the sale, and nobody is accountable for them. The outcome was agreement to move forward with continued work.

Bob and Peter shaped the next phase. Bob is a strong advocate: customers who run a multi-vendor video platform need multi-vendor assurance, CDA should be sold on vertical outcomes, and anchor accounts can be chosen from Wesco's own edge-device shipment data. Peter pressed on the two things sellers will be asked: what CDA does that the customer's video management system does not, and how pricing works at enterprise scale, where rollouts will be negotiated.

The continued work follows from that:

- An enterprise pricing construct alongside the per-site packages, including small-branch estates that cannot justify an appliance at every site.
- A one-page seller value proposition, with a CDA versus video management system comparison and two or three vertical outcome plays.
- A defined launch scope, so the sales enthusiasm lands on a specific offer.

The figures are a business case. Hardware, software and license costs are sourced; service hours, margin target and volumes are estimates; Kroger and Cisco site counts are Bob's working figures; and no customer has yet been asked to pay the fees.

## Bill Muczko's support

Bill accepted the business sponsor role in the meeting, which meets the condition you set on 2026-09-15. He leads Global Enterprise, roughly 200 Fortune 200 end users, and his stated goal is moving the sales force from pick, pack and ship toward advanced-services selling, which is the motion CDA gives a seller. His read of the market, in his words: customers "aren't managing their camera assets effectively today... If they're even monitoring it, they're using spreadsheets." He raised Walmart as a larger potential account than Kroger; it stays outside the plan until CDA is further into development.

## FY27 funding: your read

The FY27 AOP submission carries CDA's 2027 development at $246,787 and was accepted as complete in August. The investment itself is not approved. Across the five years, the business case needs $247K of development in 2027, peaks at $3.1M of cash in 2029 and turns cash positive in 2031. The two paths look like this:

| | Fund CDA as a project | Fold into the entroCIM roadmap |
|---|---|---|
| 2027 investment | $247K development, as submitted | No separate line |
| Build capacity | A CDA build in 2027 | entroCIM's 2027 roadmap, already reset to two major releases on reduced capacity |
| Commercial plan | First sites in 2028, led by Kroger | Timing re-planned around entroCIM priorities |

Either way, Bill's sponsorship, the platform (CDA runs on entroCIM and SkySpark) and the pricing and seller work carry over.
