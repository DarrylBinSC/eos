## entroCIM Update (Business Sponsor: Dirk Naylor)

Product Update

- Formalized customer training as a priced course catalog, with the foundation course available now and three additional courses launching in Q4.
- Targeted entroCIM 4 for Q1 2027: SkySpark 4 platform, containerized deployment aligned to DCOE standards, and a redesigned data center user experience.
- Secured AI Council approval for entroCIM AI, launching with entroCIM 4 to bring natural-language analytics and administration to the platform; C2P and cybersecurity review in progress, priced as an add-on capability.

- Reduced development capacity from 2026 to 2027 in line with the FY27 AOP; reconciling the 2027 roadmap to a managed cadence of two major and two maintenance releases.
