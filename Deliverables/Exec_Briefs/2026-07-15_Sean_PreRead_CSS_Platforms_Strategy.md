## What this meeting needs to decide

Two things: (1) endorsement of the direction for a broadened CSS Platforms team scope across entroCIM, WAM, the CSS AI backlog, and Navigator; (2) agreement on the principle that resources and costs follow portfolio priorities rather than legacy product lines.

## Where the portfolio stands

**Commercial.** Pipeline reported at the July 13 All Hands: approximately $24–25M total, ~$14M qualified — Federal Reserve, Dell, Nova Data Centers, Liberty Latin America, BAE. (Figures being confirmed with Nathan Kehr ahead of Wednesday.)

**WAM.** Production-ready and sellable. The DDP/EUDM enterprise-data connection went live July 3 — a realized capability milestone that establishes the governed data foundation for classification, renewal, and install-base intelligence. The next stage is converting that foundation into repeatable onboarding and measurable service outcomes. The limiting dependencies now sit across enterprise data teams (MDM structures, supplier data, stewardship) — not within the product team.

**entroCIM.** Mature platform, 10+ years in market, strong installed base; Q2 release on track for July 20. The strategic priority is not product readiness — it is converting the installed base, operational data, and delivery capability into stronger commercial performance and scalable Managed Services.

**Managed Services.** Phased operating model defined: Intelligent Facility Services first, expanding to asset, safety, remote monitoring, and site operations. A support-model realignment question surfaced July 13 and needs an owner.

**AI.** Direction question raised at the All Hands (SkySpark 4, Wooly AI, Digital Products AI Initiative) — currently unowned. Recommend it be governed within the CSS AI backlog under the portfolio structure, evaluated on measurable business value.

## The proposal

Structure the CSS Platforms organization — Product, Platforms, and Deployment — to match how the portfolio actually operates: shared product dependencies, one data foundation, one Managed Services delivery model, and budgeting that maps to portfolio priorities. This is an operating-model response to business needs, product dependencies, and accountability — it improves cost transparency and decision speed.

## What I am asking of you

1. Endorse the structural direction so detailed org and cost mapping can proceed.
2. Support the resource-alignment principle for the FY planning cycle.
3. Sponsorship on cross-functional data ownership (MDM, supplier data) — the single biggest external gate on WAM scale.
4. Ten minutes on the Akash/Dirk WAM meeting: proposed framing is WAM's data-foundation progress plus the enterprise dependencies where their support accelerates scale.

## Risk of waiting

Budget and ownership ambiguity persists across an expanded portfolio; the AI-direction and support-model decisions drift without owners; and WAM's commercialization stays gated on data dependencies no single product team can resolve.
