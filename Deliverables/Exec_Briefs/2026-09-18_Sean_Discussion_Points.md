## 2. Asset tracking: EES request (Tom Mitchell)

What was asked. Tom Mitchell's team tracks returnable assets (cable reels, reel stands) shipped to customer sites. A paired pilot of Sensolus and Alesea trackers on the same 100 assets favored Sensolus. Hemant Porwal declined the Sensolus subscription, about $5 per tracker per month or roughly $500K a year, and directed the team to build the capability internally with IT. Tom brought us in to answer whether we can. The request starts with EES and may extend to UBS as it matures.

Where I am. The application is cheap to write and not cheap to own. Our entroCIM Sensolus connector already pulls the tracker data, but the operations interface the team needs does not exist. Building it means new entroCIM PODs at roughly CDA scale, and a team kept in place to run and enhance it, about $323K a year. On the current illustrative model at an estimated 8,300 assets, five-year cost is:

| Option | Five-year cost |
|---|---|
| Sensolus subscription, as declined | $2.5M |
| Sensolus devices and services, our application | $3.6M |
| Fully Wesco-built | $6.7M |

The build saves money only if internal labor is left out. If we build, the case is strategic rather than cost avoidance: control of the data, a POD framework reused across CDA and asset tracking, and a possible sellable service with Wesco as customer zero.

Next step. Tom is arranging a technical session with Sensolus and a request for data-only pricing, and we are meeting DDP on sales order data and the tracking they have already built. The fleet size is inferred and needs a real count. That produces a partner, build or hybrid recommendation for Hemant while FY27 funding is still open.

For discussion: how to take this back to Hemant, given the numbers do not support the build direction on cost alone.
