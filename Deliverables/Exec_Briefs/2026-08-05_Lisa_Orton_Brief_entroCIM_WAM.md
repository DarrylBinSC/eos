# entroCIM and Wesco Asset Management: Status Brief

Prepared for Lisa Orton, 2026-08-05

---

## entroCIM

**The platform is delivering. The commercial conversion is not yet following.**

We shipped the Q2 release on its committed July date and the next release is set for October 5. Delivery is running to cadence, and the move off Quest to Azure completes September 1, which removes a legacy cost and risk position.

| 2026 objective | Target | Through June |
|---|---|---|
| Software sales | $6.6M | $1.1M (17%) |
| Product pull-through | $3.0M | $28K (1%) |
| Pipeline | | $32M |

The pipeline is real and executive-approved. Both revenue lines are well behind at mid-year. The constraint is conversion and attach, not product readiness or opportunity coverage.

Current sales performance has prompted a request from CSS Sales to reduce headcount in both Deployment and Development. We are working with CSS Finance and Sales to model 2027 and agree the path forward. We review the numbers with Brad Stevens this week for approval, and expect them final ahead of the August 21 AOP submission. Dirk is the named sponsor.

The 2027 plan for entroCIM is sustaining rather than expansion. The open question is what level of investment sustains the platform and the installed base while the commercial motion is rebuilt.

---

## Wesco Asset Management

**Ahead of pace to plan, led by supply chain programs.**

| 2026 objective | Target | Through June |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | $19.0M | $16.7M (87%) |

The target now includes growth from existing customers; the original measure captured new logos only.

Customer demand today sits in supply chain programs, and that is where the team is winning. License management is not the focus of those programs, and that is the right place for our commercial energy right now.

The license roadmap is delivering alongside it. License data now syncs from multiple source systems into a single view, and expiration alerts reach the owning rep this quarter, replacing account by account research with a governed renewal signal for Sales Operations. Asset and warranty coverage is being built in the same year.

Scale from here is not limited by development. It depends on supplier participation in providing license and warranty data, and on enterprise product data standards.

The FY27 submission is due August 21 alongside entroCIM.

---

**Executive takeaway.** entroCIM is executing well and selling below plan, and the 2027 resourcing model is being rebuilt around that. WAM is ahead of pace on the strength of supply chain programs. Both roadmaps continue to add capability this year: entroCIM ships its next release in October, and WAM extends from license coverage into asset and warranty data.
