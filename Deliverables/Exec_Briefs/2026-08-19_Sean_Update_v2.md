**entroCIM does not have a plan that works at any authorised revenue level.**

Finance authorised a 2027 planning range of $5M to $6M. Breakeven is approximately $7.08M, about $1.08M above the top of that range. The important part is what happens if we attack the cost side: taking development and marketing to zero together still leaves a loss of roughly $176,000. Sales cost alone exceeds gross margin.

That tells us this is not a spending problem and it will not be fixed by trimming the investment ask. Either the authorised revenue range is wrong, or the cost-to-serve structure is, and that is above my line to decide. I am submitting the plan as it stands, honestly, rather than engineering it to look breakeven.

**WAM's case is sound but the benefit cannot currently be attributed to the ask.**

WAM revenue is earned through supply chain programs that this investment does not fund. The arithmetic works, the attribution chain does not, and a reviewer will find that. I have deliberately held the payback claim at the conservative 1 to 5 year band rather than claim what I cannot defend. There are two clean routes and they lead to different priority bands, which is decision two below.

Separately, WAM's FY27 scope cannot be finalised until the scope question with John Erickson closes. It has been open since 5 August and it is the pacing item for the whole submission.

**Navigator is the strongest case in the portfolio and I want to be sure it is read that way.**

It is the only one of the four whose benefit is caused by the investment rather than correlated with it: building Navigator 2.0 is what removes the IBM Maximo renewal. It is also the only one with a dated external forcing function, since IBM is ending support and the renewal payment falls due in March or April 2027, inside the investment year. A reviewer can discount a projection. They cannot discount a bill with a date on it. If the portfolio gets trimmed, this is the one that should be hardest to cut, and I would rather that be understood in advance than argued afterwards.

**CDA has crossed the intake threshold.**

The ask is now $260,573, about $10,600 over the $250,000 line, which makes the full business case and project financials mandatory rather than optional. The business case is complete, so nothing is at risk. It does mean the submission is now a formal funding request rather than a small carryover, and it needs your endorsement on the record.

**One thing to be aware of, no action needed**

The i3 Alliance agreement as drafted would make entroCIM a downstream consumer of i3's data model rather than the system of record on Alliance projects. That is a platform positioning question, not a contract detail, and it applies to every Alliance project we take. We caught it before signature and we have a redline and a fallback position. Legal owns it from here. I flag it because if it ever came back the wrong way it would change what entroCIM is on those accounts.
