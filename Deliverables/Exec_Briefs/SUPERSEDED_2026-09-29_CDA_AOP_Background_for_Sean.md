# Connected Device Assurance

**FY27 AOP background for Sean McCloskey** · Darryl Benson · September 29, 2026

**What it is.** A managed service that keeps the connected devices Wesco sells inventoried, patched and secure, for one annual fee per site. Wesco owns the hardware and replaces it under the service agreement, so the customer commits no capital.

**Why it belongs in the portfolio.** The recurring work after a device sale currently goes to someone else or to nobody. CDA turns an installed base Wesco already owns into recurring revenue, and the data it produces feeds WAM, Navigator and entroCIM rather than standing alone.

## What the FY27 AOP is carrying

$261K of development in 2027, eight months with the existing team. No new headcount and no new platform. The hardware and software capital that follows, about $8.0M by 2031, is proportional to sites won and does not begin until deployment is approved. Peak cash across the plan is $0.8M.

## The numbers

| | Small | Medium | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual fee per site | $7,352 | $11,214 | $16,954 |
| Gross margin at that fee | 40% | 40% | 40% |
| Payback on upfront cost | 13.0 mo | 13.9 mo | 14.8 mo |

Plan to 2031: 684 sites, $10.1M of recurring revenue at a 48 percent gross margin, cash break-even in 2029.

## What sales has said

**Bill Muczko:** across roughly 200 Fortune 200 accounts, about 85 percent are not managing camera assets effectively, and where they monitor at all they use spreadsheets. He is the nominated business sponsor; acceptance is pending.

**Bob Caprise:** validation is three to five anchor accounts across different verticals, drawn from the top accounts. Kroger has already asked for a device manager at about $15 per camera, and a per-camera fee on the Axis hardware sale could fund the launch.

## What is solid, and what is not

**Solid:** the platform, connectors and secure site connection run in production today; the bill of materials is sourced, not estimated; the build is eight months with the team Wesco already funds.

**Not yet:** what a customer will actually pay, adoption inside an account, and the anchor accounts beyond Kroger. A pilot resolves these; more analysis will not.

## What is being asked

Nothing new in the AOP. Continue the FY27 development investment, and support the sponsorship ask to Bill Muczko.
