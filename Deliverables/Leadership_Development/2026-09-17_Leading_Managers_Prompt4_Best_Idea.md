# Leading Managers: Best Idea from the Execution Module

> Status: **Draft v1, 2026-09-17.** Short, in line with cohort length.

## Question

What is the best idea you obtained from our module on execution?

## Reply (draft)

The Tannenbaum-Schmidt continuum. Most delegation advice tells you to give people more room. The continuum is more useful than that, because it works in two directions: it names where I intend to operate, and it gives me a way to check where I actually am.

Those turn out to be different. I would have said I run at 5 and 6. Looking at specific handoffs, I am nearer 3 with the people whose gaps I quietly cover. The value is a scale precise enough to catch that, and plain enough to use with the team rather than just on myself.
