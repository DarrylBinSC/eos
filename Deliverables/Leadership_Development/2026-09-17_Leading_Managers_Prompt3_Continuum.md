## Question

How can you improve how you delegate work to your team members?

## Reply (draft v2)

I want to keep working at 5 and 6, giving people room to decide inside limits we set in advance. The improvement is to be explicit about it rather than leaving it to be inferred.

Two changes. When I hand work over, name the level and the limits that come with it, so nobody has to guess how much room they have. And stop moving down the continuum silently: when I fill a gap in someone's role, I have put them back at 3 without telling them. If someone needs to be at 3 for a while, that should be a conversation, not a habit.

## Superseded draft v1

I operate mostly at 5 and 6. The team defines how the work gets done and makes the call, inside limits we agree in advance: architecture standards, release gates, and what we have committed to a customer. The checks and balances are what make the freedom safe to give.

Where I slip is with individuals rather than with the team. When I fill a gap in someone's role, I have quietly moved them back to 3 without telling them. Holding the line at 6 means letting work come back imperfect and coaching it, rather than finishing it myself.
