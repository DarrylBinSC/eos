## What the program is

Wesco's leadership development program for selected managers, run as a six-month cohort drawn from across the enterprise. Delivered on Howspace, with four tabs: Welcome Page, Monthly Pre-work, Goal Acceleration, Your Cohort.

Each month covers one Wesco leadership driver:

| Month | Leadership driver |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Executes |
| 2 | Builds Talent |
| 3 | Models Integrity |
| 4 | Fosters Culture |
| 5 | Innovates |
| 6 | Ignites Change |

Month 1 (Executes) is the current module as of 2026-09-17. The welcome page does not state calendar dates for the modules; cohort posts date from roughly mid-August 2026, so the program runs to approximately February 2027. Confirm rather than rely on that.

## How it runs

- Self-driven pre-work each month in the Monthly Pre-work tab, completed before the live session.
- Live facilitated sessions scheduled by Outlook invitation. Two invitations are sent per session; only one needs to be attended.
- The Goal Acceleration tab holds a separate goal-setting tool, completed once and revisited across the program.
- Reflection section at the end of every module.

**Stated engagement model, which is what "participation" is measured by:** read what others have shared, react to the ones worth trying, and extend at least one by replying to the comment with an addition or a clarifying question. Contribution is peer-facing, not facilitator-facing.

The program's own framing: participants decide what they take from it, and should focus on what matters to them now.
