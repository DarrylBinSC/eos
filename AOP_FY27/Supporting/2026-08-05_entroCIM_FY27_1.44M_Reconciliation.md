**1. The AOP form has not been updated to the 2027 plan.** `3. BUSINESS CASE!B18` is a hardcoded 1437918 rather than a formula, so it will not follow any roster change. It currently states a 2027 investment built on a 2026 team. This is the single most consequential item on this page.

**2. Rates differ between the two documents.** The P&L uses $22/hr for India; the AOP uses $24, with one designer at $21 and one software engineer at $38. North America is $94 in both. The India delta alone moves the AOP total by roughly $40K. Agree one rate card.

Conversely the P&L carries Darryl, David Blondel and Ron Snyder at zero FTE by deliberate modeling choice ("Darryl OUT of both", "DAVID OUT", "Ron in OUT"). Neither treatment is wrong, but the two documents cannot be compared line for line without stating the bridge, and a reviewer who adds the three leaders back will get a different number.
