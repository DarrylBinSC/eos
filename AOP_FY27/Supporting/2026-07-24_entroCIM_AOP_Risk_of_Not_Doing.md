## Paste-ready text

Risk of NOT Doing

Without continued investment, entroCIM risks lost competitive differentiation, missed recurring revenue opportunities, and reduced ability to scale smart building and operational intelligence offerings. This could limit Wesco's ability to capitalize on growing customer demand for connected, data-driven facility management solutions while increasing the future cost of modernization.

- Risk of Lost Competitive Differentiation: Without continued investment, Wesco may lose the ability to differentiate through a proprietary smart facilities platform, increasing reliance on third-party solutions and giving up control of the customer experience, the customer relationship, and the operational data those relationships generate.

- Risk of Missed Revenue Growth Opportunities: Delaying or discontinuing investment could limit the ability to expand software, managed services, and recurring revenue streams tied to intelligent building and operational technology solutions.

- Risk of Losing Deployment Flexibility and Scale: entroCIM's current investment direction allows the platform to run wherever the customer requires it: in the cloud, in the customer's own environment, or on compact hardware at the customer site. This flexibility is what opens government, regulated, and security-sensitive segments that single-deployment competitors cannot serve. Without continued investment, Wesco forfeits both this reach and the platform foundation needed to scale connected facilities, energy management, and operational intelligence offerings.

- Risk of Disconnecting from the Devices Wesco Sells: Wesco sells and distributes a large and growing base of connected, converged devices. entroCIM is the capability that lets Wesco connect to, see, and stand behind those devices after the sale. Without it, Wesco's relationship with each device ends at shipment, and the pull-through, renewal, and service opportunities attached to the installed base go unrealized or go to competitors.

- Risk of a Fragmented Digital Portfolio: entroCIM provides the operational foundation behind Wesco's service offerings, including Intelligent Facility Management Services and the broader family of connected, data-driven services. A common platform lets these offerings share device connectivity, data, and operational intelligence rather than each building its own. Without continued investment, each offering must stand alone, delivery costs rise, and the portfolio loses the combined value customers cannot assemble from point products.

- Risk of Foreclosing Future Service Offerings: The next generation of recurring service offerings depends on a trusted, ongoing connection to the devices customers have already installed. entroCIM is the foundation those services will be built on. Deferring investment now raises the cost and delays the revenue of every offering that follows.
