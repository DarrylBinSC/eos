## Funding Governance — What AOP Inclusion Does and Does Not Mean (added 2026-09-17, Darryl direct)

**A line in an AOP is a request, not a funded commitment.** Building a number into the annual operating plan places it in the ask. It still has to clear the approval process and exist as a project in the UiPath system before anything is funded. Until both have happened, the correct description is "intended to be requested in the FY__ AOP," never "budgeted," "funded," or "has a budget home."

**Why this is written down.** On 2026-09-17 a watch-list finding that an $84,000 Strev AI commitment had no budget home was recorded as narrowed because Amy Aguirre stated she was including the figure in the WAM AOP. That was wrong: the exposure is materially unchanged until the approval clears and the UiPath project exists. Darryl corrected it the same day. The same error is available on every AOP-cycle item in the portfolio, so the test applies portfolio-wide.

**The test to apply.** Before describing anything as funded, name which of the three states it is in: (1) a number requested in an AOP; (2) approved through the funding process; (3) a live project in UiPath. State the one that is true. Do not let (1) be reported upward as (2) or (3).
